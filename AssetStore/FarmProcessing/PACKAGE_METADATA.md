## Description — направление текста, не готовые утверждения

Create farm processing areas for cozy farming and crafting games. The planned collection covers cheese and butter making, fruit crushing and juice pressing, grain milling, jam cooking and food drying, with bakery, seed oil, smoking and honey equipment in the full scope.

Planned full delivery: 72 individual prop families, including 12 production stations, plus 8 reusable workshop compositions and 2 demo scenes. Specialized tools, intermediate products, furniture and a small modular workshop kit complete each work area.

The product is planned as a standalone art pack. No other farm package is required. Selected mechanical parts are separated for your own animation. Production logic, inventory, recipes, economy, characters and animation clips are outside the planned release.
