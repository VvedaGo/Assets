FARM MARKET STARTER LITE  (v1.0.0)
Free stylized low-poly farm market props.

REQUIREMENTS
- Unity 6 (6000.3 or newer), Universal Render Pipeline (URP).
- The demo scene camera uses the Input System package (com.unity.inputsystem).
  The props, materials and prefabs work without it.

QUICK START
1. Open Demo/Scenes/Demo_Lite_Showcase.unity to see everything arranged as a market corner.
   In Play mode the camera orbits automatically; drag with the left mouse button, scroll to zoom,
   press Tab to switch to the prop gallery.
2. Drag any prefab from Art/Prefabs/Single into your scene.
3. Art/Prefabs/Compositions/FMS_Comp_VegetableStand is a ready-made, filled vegetable stand.

FOLDERS
Art/Models        FBX meshes, grouped by category (Veg, Fruit, Crate, Stall, Deco)
Art/Materials     URP/Lit materials used by the props
Art/Textures      Base color textures
Art/Prefabs       Single props and compositions
Demo              Demo scene and its helper assets. Safe to delete if you do not need it.
Documentation     This file and the changelog

PREFABS (22)
Naming: FMS_<Category>_<Name>_<Variant>
Veg:     Tomato_Whole, Tomato_Half, Tomato_Branch, Carrot, Potato, Cabbage, Pumpkin
Fruit:   Apple, Watermelon
Crate:   Framed_Open, Framed_Closed, Slatted_Empty, Basket_Large, Basket_Small
Stall:   Table_Canopy, Table_Open, Cart_Empty, Scale, Weights
Deco:    Signboard_Chalk, Signs_Hanging
Comp:    FMS_Comp_VegetableStand

NOTES
- Scale: 1 unit = 1 meter. Pivot at the base of each object.
- Every prefab has a collider. There are no LODs in the Lite pack.
- Built for URP. For Built-in or HDRP, convert materials with the standard Unity material upgrade tools.
