# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — Initial release

- 88 individual FBX models and 88 categorized art prefabs.
- Seven crops in three growth stages and three wheat field tiles.
- Trees, garden beds, flowers, foliage, ground, paths, water, rocks and wood details.
- 27 art materials and 27 Base Color textures.
- One organized showcase scene with overview, six local scene studies and category collection.
- One enabled overview camera; production shot cameras and icon-only vignette excluded from the release scene.
- Optional demo URP settings, Getting Started window, documentation and measured model inventory.

Static art only. No LODs, rigs, animations or farming gameplay system.
