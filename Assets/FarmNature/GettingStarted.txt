FARM NATURE
Low Poly Crops, Trees & Landscape
Version 1.0.0

START HERE
1. Use Unity 6000.3.10f1 and Universal Render Pipeline 17.3.0.
2. Open Tools > Farm Nature > Getting Started.
3. Click Open Showcase Scene, then use Overview / Scene Studies / Collection
   to frame the corresponding group in the Scene view.
4. Press Play to see the overview camera. This is a static art demo;
   no player controller or gameplay system is required.

If your scene materials are pink, check that URP is installed and active.
For a fresh project, Demo/Settings contains an optional demo pipeline.
Assign it in Project Settings > Graphics and the active Quality level.
Existing URP projects can keep their own pipeline. Importing this pack
does not overwrite GraphicsSettings or QualitySettings.
