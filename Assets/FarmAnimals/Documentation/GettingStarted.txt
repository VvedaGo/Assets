FARM ANIMALS - Low Poly Livestock, Poultry & Pets (Rigged & Animated)
=====================================================================

Requirements
- Unity 6 (6000.3 or newer), Universal Render Pipeline (URP 17).
- No other packages. No code dependencies in the prefabs except the small FarmAnimalEvents component.

Contents
- 35 animals, each a rigged FBX (Generic rig) with its own 256 px flat-colour palette atlas and URP/Lit material:
  Livestock (LIV): Cow, Calf, Bull, Horse, Foal, Donkey, Sheep, Lamb, Ram, Goat, Kid, Pig, Piglet
  Birds (BRD):     Hen, Rooster, Chick, Turkey, Duck, Duckling, Goose, Pigeon, Sparrow, Crow
  Pets (PET):      Dog, Puppy, Cat, Kitten
  Small (SML):     Fox, Rabbit, Hedgehog, Mouse, Frog
  Insects (INS):   Bee, Butterfly, Ladybug (shown larger than life so they read on a game camera)
- 113 animation clips (2-3 per animal, 14 for the cow), 30 fps, in place (no root motion).
- 35 ready prefabs: Art/Prefabs/<Category>/FMS_<Animal>.prefab
- 8 compositions: Art/Prefabs/CMP/FMS_CMP_<Name>.prefab
  Pasture, Stable, SheepFlock, Pigsty, Barnyard, Pond, Garden, Orchard
- 2 demo scenes: Demo/Scenes/Demo_FarmAnimals_Showcase.unity and Demo_FarmAnimals_Compositions.unity
- Documentation/ImportReport.txt lists every animal with its triangles, clips, events and size.

Folder layout (Assets/FarmAnimals)
  Art/Models      FBX files (clips are inside each FBX)
  Art/Textures    palette atlases (Point filter, no mipmaps, uncompressed)
  Art/Materials   one URP/Lit material per animal
  Art/Animators   one Animator Controller per animal
  Art/Prefabs     animal prefabs and compositions
  Scripts         FarmAnimalEvents (runtime, assembly FarmAnimals.Runtime)
  Demo            demo scenes, demo materials/meshes, FarmAnimalDemo script (assembly FarmAnimals.Demo)

Using an animal
1. Drag a prefab from Art/Prefabs into the scene. It has an Animator, a CapsuleCollider and FarmAnimalEvents.
2. Drive the Animator Controller from your code:
   - "Speed" (float, m/s): Idle -> Walk -> Run/Trot blend tree for animals that move on the ground.
     The thresholds are the real speed of each clip, so moving the transform at that speed keeps the feet from sliding.
   - One bool per extra clip, named like the clip: Graze, Eat, Drink, Lie_Idle, Sit_Idle, Peck, Swim, Fly, Root,
     Browse, Nibble, Play_Hop, Play_Bow, Pounce, Display, Crow, Paw_Ground, Curl_Up, Jump, ...
     Set it to true to play that clip, false to return to Idle/Locomotion.
   - Animals without a ground walk (Bee, Butterfly) start in their first clip (Fly) and switch with the bools.
   - Example:  animator.SetFloat("Speed", 0.9f);  animator.SetBool("Graze", true);
3. Clip names are <Animal>_<Clip>, e.g. Cow_Walk. Loop clips loop; one-shot clips (Cow: Hit_React, Lie_Down, Stand_Up,
   Moo) play once.

Animation events (sounds are not included)
The clips carry events: Footstep (parameter = leg, e.g. "FrontL"), Vocal, LayEgg, Peck, Nibble, Sniff, Paw, Pounce,
Jump, Land, Takeoff, Flap, WingFlap, Paddle, Curl, Display. FarmAnimalEvents receives them and raises C# events:
   var events = GetComponent<FarmAnimals.FarmAnimalEvents>();
   events.FootstepRaised += leg => audioSource.PlayOneShot(stepClip);
   events.AnimationEventRaised += (name, parameter) => Debug.Log(name);
Keep the component on the prefab root (next to the Animator); without it Unity logs "AnimationEvent has no receiver".

Demo scenes
- Demo_FarmAnimals_Showcase: every animal on a floating island per category; each one cycles through all its clips.
- Demo_FarmAnimals_Compositions: the 8 compositions; ground animals wander around their island.
- Press Play to see the animations. Each island has a disabled shot camera (Cam_<Name>) for screenshots.
- FarmAnimalDemo is demo code only; delete the Demo folder if you do not need it.

Technical details
- Rig: Generic avatar per animal, 15-29 bones; bones stay visible in the hierarchy (attach props to "Head" etc.).
- Meshes: cow, bull, calf, horse, foal and donkey are one skinned mesh; the other animals are built from 16-33
  skinned part meshes that share one material.
- Triangles: 400 - 2400 per animal (see ImportReport.txt). No LODs.
- Materials: URP/Lit, one 256x256 atlas per animal, GPU instancing enabled.
- Scale: 1 unit = 1 m, Y up, the models face +Z, the pivot is on the ground.
- Colliders: one CapsuleCollider per animal, fitted to the rest pose (it does not follow the animation).
