FARM ANIMALS - CHANGELOG

1.0.0 (2026-11-25)
- Initial release.
- 35 rigged low-poly animals (Generic rig and avatar) and 35 animal prefabs:
  livestock 13, birds 10, pets 4, small animals 5, insects 3.
- 113 in-place animation clips (30 fps) with 182 animation events.
- 35 Animator Controllers (Locomotion blend tree by Speed + one bool per extra clip).
- FarmAnimalEvents component that re-raises the clip events as C# events.
- 8 composition prefabs: Pasture, Stable, SheepFlock, Pigsty, Barnyard, Pond, Garden, Orchard.
- 2 demo scenes (Showcase, Compositions) with the FarmAnimalDemo script.
- 35 palette atlases (256 x 256) and 35 URP/Lit materials.
